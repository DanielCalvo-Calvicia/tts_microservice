## Goal

Check that the deployed service runs on **Piper**, not on the fallback, and that the audio it returns has the format Brain asks for. This is the check `/available` cannot do (F5): it is read from the log.

## Read first

[../../../deployment/deployment.md](../../../deployment/deployment.md) ("Verification after a deploy"), [../../../operations/monitoring.md](../../../operations/monitoring.md) (the log lines), [../../../api/api.md](../../../api/api.md).

## Files

None changed.

## Steps

1. Read the log: `py -3 oblivion.py logs --host pc -s tts -n 200`. It must contain `Piper voice loaded` and `TtsService initialized` with `engine=PiperSpeechSynthesis`, and must **not** contain `Piper unavailable; falling back to pyttsx3`.
2. `GET http://<tts address>:8002/health` answers `200` with `healthy: true`; `GET /available` answers `is_available: true`.
3. `POST /process/batch?text=hello&sample_rate=24000` answers `200` with base64 audio, `sample_rate` 24000 and `channels` 1. Decode `audio_data_base64` (`data` of `ProcessBatchResponse`; `bytes_base64` is the name inside stream events only) and check the length is not 0 (PCM16 mono: at least a few thousand bytes for one word).
4. `POST /process/stream?sample_rate=24000&channels=1` with the text `hello` as the body answers `200` with headerless PCM16.
5. Blank text: `POST /process/batch?text=` answers `400`.

The `set`/`get` stream pair is covered by T-01.1.6 and by the pipeline tests of `contracts`; do not repeat it here.

## Acceptance criteria

All five steps give the stated result. If step 1 shows the fallback line, the task is still `done` as a check, and the finding goes into *Notes* and into T-01.1.4's drill as a real case.

## Verification

The log lines found and the status codes, in *Notes*.

### Result (run on 2026-11-06 against the tts deployed by T-01.1.2, `http://127.0.0.1:8002`, native, Windows)

1. **Log** (`oblivion.py logs --host robot --config <throwaway copy> -s tts -n 200`, 18 lines): contains `Piper voice loaded voice="en_GB-alan-medium" sample_rate=22050` (from `infrastructure.outbound.piper_speech.piper_engine`) and `TtsService initialized name="TTS Microservice" engine="PiperSpeechSynthesis"`. No line with `falling back` and none with `unavailable`. The log field is written `engine="PiperSpeechSynthesis"` (quoted). Pass.
2. **`GET /health`:** `200`, `{"action":"health_check","status":"success","status_code":200,"message":"Service is healthy",...,"data":{"healthy":true}}`. **`GET /available`:** `200`, `data` = `{"is_available":true,"reason":null}`. Pass. `reason` is `null`: it is the field T-01.2 proposes to use.
3. **`POST /process/batch?text=hello&sample_rate=24000`:** `200`, `data` = `{audio_data_base64, sample_rate: 24000, channels: 1}`; decoded audio **30,286 bytes** (about 0.63 s of PCM16 mono at 24 kHz), 25,214 of them non-zero. Without `sample_rate` the answer is 22050 / 1 (the default). Pass. (The task text said `bytes_base64`; the field of the batch answer is `audio_data_base64`, as `docs/api/api.md` says: corrected in step 3 above.)
4. **`POST /process/stream?sample_rate=24000&channels=1`** with body `hello`: `200`, `Content-Type: audio/wav`, **28,796 bytes**, starting with sample bytes `252,255,0,0,6,0,...` (no `RIFF` header): headerless PCM16 labelled `audio/wav`, as documented (debt D-3). Pass.
5. **`POST /process/batch?text=`:** `400`. Pass.

All five steps give the stated result, on the Piper path.

- Batch and stream lengths differ by about 1.5 KB for the same word (30,286 vs 28,796 bytes); not investigated, the audio is not empty and both are plausible for "hello" (different code paths, not a requirement).
- No new contradiction with the documents, apart from the field name in this task file (fixed).
